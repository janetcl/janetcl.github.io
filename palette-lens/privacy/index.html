Palette Lens privacy policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Palette Lens is a browser extension that labels product listings by how well their colours suit the colour season you choose.

What it stores

Your chosen season, display mode (hide, dim or badge), match strictness, and the list of sites you've switched it off on. These are saved in your browser's extension storage, which your browser may sync between your own signed-in devices. Palette Lens has no server and never receives them.

What it reads

On pages you visit, Palette Lens looks for product photos and reads their pixels to work out each garment's colour. It fetches those images from the same addresses the page already loads them from, analyses them in your browser, keeps the result in memory only while the browser is running, and sends nothing anywhere.

What it doesn't do

No accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or cookies. It doesn't collect, sell or share personal information, browsing history or page content.
