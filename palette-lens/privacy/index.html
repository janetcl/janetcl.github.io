Palette Lens privacy policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Palette Lens is a browser extension that labels product listings by how well their colours suit the colour season you choose.

What it stores

Your chosen season, display mode (hide, dim or badge), match strictness, and the list of sites you've switched it on for. These are saved in your browser's extension storage, which your browser may sync between your own signed-in devices. Palette Lens has no server and never receives them.

What it reads

Palette Lens is off on every site until you switch it on for that site. On sites you've switched it on for, it looks for product photos and reads their pixels to work out each garment's colour. It fetches those images from the same addresses the page already loads them from, analyses them in your browser, keeps the result in memory only while the browser is running, and sends nothing anywhere.

Camera

The “find your season” page can use your camera, only when you press “Turn on camera” and allow it. It reads the colour of your skin and hair from the picture in your browser to suggest a season. The picture is never saved, uploaded or sent anywhere, and the camera turns off as soon as the reading is done. If you choose the suggested season, only the season's name is saved, like any other season you pick.

What it doesn't do

No accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or cookies. It doesn't collect, sell or share personal information, browsing history or page content.
